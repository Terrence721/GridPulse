GridPulse — architecture note

Phase 1 proved the domain logic. Phase 2 replaced the transport underneath it.

Meter Simulator → Usage Aggregation → Billing shipped first over direct REST calls, deliberately — get the idempotency and rollup logic right against a synchronous request/response before adding a message broker's own failure modes on top. Phase 2 swapped the transport for real Kafka events through a real Confluent Schema Registry, with no change to the domain logic itself.

Why event-driven, not a bigger REST surface: independent scaling of ingestion vs. billing, a natural audit trail of events, and — proven twice now, not just argued for — the ability to add a new consumer of an existing topic without touching its producer. Notification Service and the Stripe payment consumer are both real, live examples of that exact property, not a hypothetical benefit from the design doc.
